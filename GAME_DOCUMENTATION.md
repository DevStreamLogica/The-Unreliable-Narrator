# Game Overview

## What is DSA?

**The Unreliable Narrator** is a horror murder mystery game set in **Vance Manor**, a grand estate that holds dark secrets spanning decades. The story centers on the murder of Harold Vance on **November 15th, 1987**.

## Genre & Atmosphere

- **Genre:** Horror Murder Mystery (Text Adventure → LibGDX First-Person Navigation)
- **Tone:** Haunting, mysterious, psychological horror
- **Themes:** Betrayal, greed, legacy, entrapment, cycles of death

## The Premise

You are an investigator who has come to Vance Manor to solve the murder of Harold Vance. But this is no ordinary investigation. You are guided by a mysterious **Narrator** - a trapped soul who channels the memories of suspects, reliving the events of that fateful night in 1987.

As you dig deeper, you realize that the manor itself is watching. Something ancient and malevolent dwells within these walls - and every question you ask, every room you enter, draws its attention closer.

# The World & Setting

## Vance Manor

Vance Manor is a grand Victorian estate built in the early 1900s. The main house has two floors plus a cellar, with the Groundskeeper's Shed located on the exterior grounds.

**Architectural Style:** Victorian Gothic Revival  
**Built:** Circa 1902  
**Location:** Rural estate, outside of town  
**Current State (1987):** Well-maintained exterior, dark history within

## The Investigation

The game takes place in a liminal space between past and present. The player experiences the manor as it was in 1987, guided by memories channeled through the Narrator. The house itself is a character - watching, waiting, aware of your presence.

# Timeline of Events

## 1957 - The Founding & The Betrayal

- **Vance Pharmaceutical** is co-founded by **Harold Vance** and **Thomas Vance**
- Thomas was "the real chemist" - the genius behind the formulas
- Harold was "just the money man" - the face of the company
- **Harold entombed Thomas ALIVE behind the study wall** to take full control
- Official records state Thomas "retired abroad" - but no one ever saw him leave

## 1957-1987 - Thirty Years of Darkness

- Thomas remains trapped behind the wall for 30 years
- Through decades of tortured existence, Thomas **becomes something inhuman**
- Thomas **fuses with the house itself**, becoming the Entity
- The manor becomes a place of dark energy and trapped souls

## November 15th, 1987 - The Murder

- **Harold Vance is murdered** in his study
- Multiple suspects were present at the manor that night
- **James Harlow** (the groundskeeper) arrived at 6:02 PM - two hours early
- **Daniel Marsh** found the body
- The investigation begins

## 1987 (After the Murder) - Arthur's Investigation

- **Arthur Hollis**, a private investigator, comes to solve the case
- He conducts interviews with all suspects and records them on tapes
- He hides the tapes around the house (sensing something is wrong)
- He discovers old records of other deaths, other investigators who came before
- He finds construction documents about Thomas's hidden space
- **Arthur finds the seam behind the bookshelf**
- **Arthur opens the wall**
- **Arthur releases the Entity**
- Arthur becomes the Entity's first victim after its release

## 1987-Present - The Cycle

- Other investigators come to Vance Manor
- Each one investigates, watches the tapes, and dies
- Each death creates a new Narrator, replacing the previous one
- The current Narrator is the most recent failure
- **You are the newest investigator** - and possibly the last hope

# Key Characters

## Harold Vance (The Victim)

### Who He Was
Harold Vance was the wealthy owner of Vance Manor and co-founder of Vance Pharmaceutical. To the public, he was a successful businessman and philanthropist. In reality, he was a ruthless man who built his empire on betrayal.

### What He Did
- Co-founded Vance Pharmaceutical with his partner Thomas Vance in 1957
- **Entombed Thomas alive behind the study wall** to steal the company
- Fabricated the story that Thomas "retired abroad"
- Cut out his own sister Margaret from the family
- Planned to terminate his loyal assistant Daniel
- Created enemies through decades of manipulation and greed

### Role in the Story
Harold is the murder victim. His past sins - particularly the entombment of Thomas - set all the dark events in motion. Even in death, his betrayals continue to echo through the manor.

---

## Thomas Vance (The Entity)

### Who He Was
Thomas Vance was Harold's business partner and the true genius behind Vance Pharmaceutical. He was "the real chemist" - the man who created the formulas that made the company successful.

### What Happened
- Co-founded Vance Pharmaceutical in 1957
- Created the formulas that made the company valuable
- Trusted Harold, his partner
- **Was entombed alive behind the study wall by Harold in 1957**
- Spent 30 years trapped in darkness
- **Transformed into the Entity** through suffering and rage
- Now watches all who enter the manor
- Consumes investigators who dig too deep

### Role in the Story
Thomas IS the Entity. The **Awareness Meter** in the game represents Thomas becoming aware of the player's presence. At 80 awareness, Thomas fully awakens and the investigation ends. He is the true horror lurking within Vance Manor - a victim who became a monster.

---

## Arthur Hollis (The First Investigator)

### Who He Was
Arthur Hollis was a private investigator who came to Vance Manor in 1987 to solve Harold's murder. He was thorough, methodical, and ultimately doomed.

### What He Did
- Investigated the Vance murder in 1987
- Conducted interviews with all five suspects
- **Recorded interviews on videotapes** and hid them around the manor
- Discovered old photographs and names he didn't recognize
- Found records of other deaths, other investigators before him
- Discovered that Thomas Vance built a hidden space
- Found a seam behind the bookshelf in the study
- **Opened the wall and released the Entity**
- Became the Entity's first victim after its release

### Role in the Story
Arthur's tapes are scattered throughout the manor and provide crucial evidence. Tape 6 (Arthur's Log) contains his final recordings before opening the wall. His actions released Thomas/the Entity, beginning the cycle of death that continues to this day.

---

## The Narrator (The Most Recent Victim)

### Who They Are
The Narrator is the consciousness that guides the player through the investigation. They are NOT Arthur Hollis - they are simply the most recent investigator who died in Vance Manor.

### What They Do
- Guide the player through the investigation
- "Channel" the memories of suspects (this is how interviews work)
- Provide information about locations and evidence
- Give warnings when awareness rises
- Slowly remember their own fate as the investigation progresses
- Experience confusion, fear, and desperation as memories return

### Role in the Story
The Narrator is the player's companion and guide. Through them, players experience the story. Their growing awareness of their own trapped state adds emotional weight to the investigation. They represent what the player will become if they fail.

### The Succession of Narrators
1. Thomas was entombed, contained the entity
2. Arthur opened the wall, released the entity, died, became first narrator
3. Other investigators came, died, replaced previous narrators
4. Current narrator is the most recent failure
5. The player is the newest investigator

### Personality Shifts (Based on Awareness)
- **Early Game:** Hopeful, eager to help, clear-minded
- **Mid Game:** Growing confusion, occasional hesitations, fragmented thoughts
- **Late Game:** Increasing urgency, fear, fragmented sentences, growing desperation

# The Suspects

All five suspects were present at Vance Manor on the night of November 15th, 1987. Each had motive, means, and opportunity.

---

## Edward Cross (The Business Partner)

### Who He Is
Edward Cross was Harold's business partner for 30 years. Together they built Vance Pharmaceutical - or so Edward believed.

### Cooperation Level
**Starting Cooperation: 70** (Moderate - willing to talk but cautious)

### Personality
- Professional, reserved
- Deeply hurt by betrayal
- Values loyalty and partnership
- Speaks of the past with bitterness

### What He Did
- Helped build Vance Pharmaceutical over 30 years
- Believed Harold was his partner
- Discovered Harold was planning to take everything
- Present at the manor the night of the murder

### Key Quotes
- *"Thirty years. We built that company together. And he was going to take it all."*

### Motive
Harold was going to betray Edward after 30 years of partnership, stealing the company they built together.

---

## Daniel Marsh (The Assistant)

### Who He Is
Daniel Marsh served as Harold's personal assistant for 8 years. Loyal and hardworking, he believed his dedication would be rewarded. He was wrong.

### Cooperation Level
**Starting Cooperation: 85** (High - wants to help, nothing to hide... or so it seems)

### Personality
- Conflicted, emotional
- Believed in loyalty
- Feels betrayed and set up
- Complex motivations - didn't want Harold dead

### What He Did
- Served Harold loyally for 8 years
- **Found Harold's body** on November 15th, 1987
- Was being terminated and framed by Harold (the Termination Letter)
- Someone warned James about Harold's speech

### Key Quotes
- *"I served him for eight years. I thought loyalty meant something. It didn't."*
- *"I didn't want him dead. I just wanted... I wanted him to know what it felt like."*

### Motive
Daniel was being fired and framed by Harold. The termination letter proves Harold was setting Daniel up - giving him real, personal motive for murder.

### Evidence Against Daniel
- **Daniel's Note** - Guest rooms, in a coat pocket
- **Tape 4** - Daniel's interview (under the desk in the Study)
- **Termination Letter** - Study drawers

---

## Margaret Vance (The Sister)

### Who She Is
Margaret Vance is Harold's younger sister. Once close to her brother, she was cut out of the family when money changed Harold.

### Cooperation Level
**Starting Cooperation: 75** (Moderate-High - has pain to share)

### Personality
- Deeply wounded by family betrayal
- Nostalgic for childhood bond with Harold
- Isolated and cut off from family
- Carries pain of abandonment

### What She Did
- Grew up close to Harold
- Watched her brother change as wealth corrupted him
- Was cut out of the family by Harold
- Present at the manor the night of the murder

### Key Quotes
- *"We were close once. When we were children. Before the money changed him."*
- *"He was my brother. My only family. And he cut me out like I was nothing."*

### Motive
Harold abandoned Margaret, cutting her out of the family completely. The pain of losing her only family gave her reason to want revenge.

---

## Victor Eames (The Investigator)

### Who He Is
Victor Eames knew things about Harold Vance - dark things. He knew about the deaths connected to the company, and he did nothing.

### Cooperation Level
**Starting Cooperation: 60** (Lowest - has secrets to protect)

### Personality
- Morally complex
- Guilty and conflicted
- Knows more than he wants to admit
- Dangerous topic: **Tape 7** (Victor's Recording)

### What He Did
- Investigated Harold's business dealings
- Discovered deaths connected to Vance Pharmaceutical
- **Did nothing about what he found**
- Kept Harold's secrets
- Present at the manor the night of the murder

### Key Quotes
- *"I knew about the deaths. I did nothing. I'm as guilty as he was."*

### Motive
Victor's guilt made him complicit in Harold's crimes. Perhaps he wanted to silence the only person who knew what he had done - or failed to do.

### Special Note
Discussing Victor and Tape 7 together is a **Dangerous Topic** that costs extra awareness (+5).

## James Harlow (The Groundskeeper)

### Who He Is
James Harlow has been the groundskeeper at Vance Manor for 29 years. Patient, loyal, waiting for something that never came.

### Cooperation Level
**Starting Cooperation: 90** (Highest - appears helpful and cooperative)

### Personality
- Deeply emotional beneath calm exterior
- Waited nearly 30 years for something
- Feels foolish for hoping
- Vulnerable and rejected

### What He Did
- Worked as groundskeeper for 29 years
- **Arrived at the manor at 6:02 PM** - two hours early (and lied about it)
- Someone warned him about Harold's speech
- Present at the manor the night of the murder

### Key Quotes
- *"Twenty-nine years. That's how long I waited. For nothing."*
- *"I thought maybe this time... but I was a fool."*

### Motive
James waited 29 years for something (possibly recognition, inheritance, or acknowledgment) and received nothing. His arrival two hours early suggests premeditation.

### Evidence Against James
- **Groundskeeper's Log** - Proves he arrived at 6:02 PM, not when he claimed
- **Speech Notes** - Study papers
- **Tape 3** - Victor's interview (Parlor clock)
- **Tape 5** - James's interview (Shed shelf)

# The Entity & Awareness System

## What It Is

The Entity is **Thomas Vance** - transformed by 30 years of suffering into something no longer human. It is not a separate creature; it IS Thomas, fused with the house itself.

## How It Works

The Entity's awareness of the player is tracked through the **Awareness System**:

| Action | Awareness Cost |
|--------|----------------|
| Move to location | +1 |
| Look around | +1 |
| Examine anything | +1 |
| Ask narrator | +1 |
| Start interview | +1 |
| Interview question | +1 |
| Present evidence | +2 |
| Confrontation with evidence | +3 |
| Dangerous topic (Victor + Tape 7) | +5 |
| Watch tape | +4 |
| Wrong accusation | +15 |

## Awareness Thresholds

| Awareness | State | What Happens |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| 0-19 | DORMANT | Entity sleeps, investigation is safe |
| 20-39 | STIRRING | Entity begins to stir, warnings appear |
| 40-59 | WATCHING | Entity is watching, random atmospheric events |
| 60-79 | HUNTING | Entity actively searches, intense warnings |
| 80+ | AWAKENED | Game Over - investigation phase ends |

## Environmental Effects

As awareness increases, the manor itself responds:

**Low Awareness (20-39):**
- *"A draft from nowhere. The temperature dropped. Just for a moment."*
- *"Was that book always open? You don't remember seeing it before."*

**Medium Awareness (40-59):**
- *"Something shifts in the shadows. Or was that just the house settling?"*
- *"You hear something. A whisper? No. Just your imagination."*

**High Awareness (60+):**
- *"You feel watched. Like something is standing just behind you."*
- *"The study feels smaller. Like the walls are closer."*

# Locations & Map

## Available Locations

### Entrance Hall
The main entrance of Vance Manor. Grand, imposing, and the threshold between the outside world and the horrors within.

### The Study (Crime Scene)
Harold's private study where he was murdered. His desk still sits where he died, chair slightly turned. This is where Thomas was entombed behind the wall in 1957.

**Contains:**
- Letter opener (missing - the murder weapon?)
- Termination letter (evidence against Daniel)
- Speech notes (evidence against James)
- Hidden tapes (Tape 1, Tape 4)
- The bookshelf that hides Thomas's tomb

### The Parlor
Where guests gathered after dinner on the night of the murder. The grandfather clock is stopped at 11:47 - the time Daniel found the body.

**Contains:**
- Victor's research/briefcase
- Hidden tapes (Tape 2, Tape 3, Tape 7)

### The Kitchen
Large, industrial kitchen. Includes access to the storage cellar.

**Contains:**
- Hidden tape (Tape 6 - Arthur's Log)

### Guest Rooms
The guest wing where Margaret and James stayed. Personal belongings reveal secrets.

**Contains:**
- Margaret's letter
- Daniel's note (evidence against Daniel)

### Groundskeeper's Shed
Small exterior building where James Harlow works. Contains evidence of his early arrival.

**Contains:**
- Groundskeeper's log (evidence against James)
- Hidden tape (Tape 5)

### Servants' Quarters
Staff area with narrow, hidden passages. The way servants moved unseen - the way someone could still move unseen.

### The Cellar
Dark storage area beneath the kitchen. Flour sacks and wine racks. Contains Tape 6.

# The Tapes

Arthur Hollis recorded interviews with all five suspects and hid the tapes around Vance Manor before the Entity consumed him. Each tape provides crucial evidence and insight into what happened the night of November 15th, 1987.

## Tape Overview

| Tape | Subject | Interview/Recording Location | Hidden Location | How to Find |
|------|---------|------------------------------|-----------------|-------------|
| Tape 1 | Edward Cross | Edward's Office (Downtown) | Study Bookshelves | `examine bookshelves` (x2) |
| Tape 2 | Margaret Vance | Margaret's Hotel Suite | Parlor Fireplace | `examine fireplace` then `examine brick` |
| Tape 3 | Victor Eames | Local Diner | Parlor Clock | `examine clock` (x2) |
| Tape 4 | Daniel Marsh | Police Station | Under Study Desk | `examine under desk` |
| Tape 5 | James Harlow | Manor Grounds (Garden Bench) | Shed Shelf | `examine shelf` |
| Tape 6 | Arthur's Final Recording | The Study (Day 5 - His Death) | Kitchen Cellar | `examine cellar` then `examine flour` |
| Tape 7 | Victor's Recording | The Parlor (Night of Murder) | Parlor Briefcase | `examine briefcase` (x2) |

**Important Notes:**
- **Tape 6** is not an interview - it captures Arthur's final moments as he opens the wall and encounters the Entity (Thomas). This tape triggers the game's climax.
- **Tape 7** was recorded by Victor himself (not Arthur) the night of the murder. Discussing it with Victor costs +5 awareness.
- In 1987, the house wasn't cursed yet - Arthur conducted interviews at neutral locations outside the manor.

## Tape 6 - The Climax Trigger

Tape 6 is Arthur's final recording. It starts as a simple investigation log and ends with Arthur's death. On this tape, Arthur:
- Discovers records of other investigators who never left
- Finds construction documents about Thomas's hidden space
- Finds the seam behind the bookshelf
- Opens the wall
- Encounters Thomas (who pretends to be a victim)
- Realizes Thomas is no longer human
- Screams as the recording ends

**WARNING:** Playing Tape 6 triggers the game's climax sequence. The Narrator begins remembering their own fate, and the Entity fully awakens.

# Game Mechanics

## Core Systems

### Navigation System (LibGDX Implementation)
- **First-Person View:** Player sees current room from first-person perspective
- **Clickable Hotspots:** Arrows and doors allow navigation between rooms
- **Keyboard Controls:** WASD or arrow keys for movement
- **Room Transitions:** Smooth transitions between locations

### Evidence Collection
- Physical evidence found by examining objects
- Hidden tapes scattered throughout the manor
- Evidence automatically added to inventory
- Evidence required for making accusations

### Suspect Interviews
- Talk to suspects through the Narrator
- Narrator "channels" suspect memories
- Cooperation system: suspects have patience levels
- Push too hard without evidence = cooperation drops
- At 0% cooperation, suspect shuts down permanently

### Awareness System
- Every action increases awareness
- Awareness represents the Entity (Thomas) noticing you
- At 80 awareness, Entity awakens = Game Over
- Balance thoroughness with awareness management

### Contradiction System
- Ask about the same topics multiple times
- Narrator's memory is unreliable
- Contradictions reveal the truth
- Need at least 1 contradiction to make accusation

## Commands (Text Version)

- `look` - Describe current location
- `go to [location]` - Travel somewhere
- `examine [object]` - Look at something closely
- `ask about [topic]` - Ask the narrator about something
- `talk to [suspect]` - Interview a suspect
- `show [evidence]` - Show evidence to current suspect
- `play tape [#]` - Watch a collected videotape
- `notebook` - View clues and contradictions
- `inventory` - View collected evidence
- `accuse` - Make an accusation (when ready)
- `status` - Check awareness level
- `hint` - Get a hint on what to do next
- `save [name]` - Save game
- `load [name]` - Load game
- `quit` - End game

## Accusation Requirements

To successfully accuse James and Daniel, you need:

**Evidence Against James (need 2 of 4):**
- Groundskeeper's Log
- Speech Notes
- Tape 3 (watched)
- Tape 5 (watched)

**Evidence Against Daniel (need 1 of 3):**
- Daniel's Note
- Tape 4 (watched)
- Termination Letter

**Also Required:**
- At least 1 contradiction (ask about weapon or body position twice)

# The Solution

## The Killers

**James Harlow and Daniel Marsh** worked together to kill Harold Vance.

### How It Happened

1. **Daniel's Plan:**
   - Daniel discovered Harold was going to fire and frame him
   - Daniel found out about Harold's cruel speech that would publicly humiliate James
   - Daniel called James a week before the dinner, warning him
   - Daniel sent James a note telling him where to find the speech (third drawer)
   - Daniel wanted James to see the speech and become angry enough to kill Harold

2. **James's Actions:**
   - James arrived at 6:02 PM - two hours early
   - James found the speech notes in the third drawer (as Daniel's note said)
   - James read the speech and realized Harold was going to publicly deny his existence
   - At 10:45 PM, James went to the study
   - Harold was there, alone
   - James confronted Harold
   - James killed Harold with the letter opener

3. **The Cover-Up:**
   - The window was opened from inside to make it look like a break-in
   - No one actually went through the window (no footprints despite rain)
   - The murder was staged to look like a burglary

### Why They Did It

- **James:** After 29 years of waiting for recognition, Harold was going to publicly deny James's existence. The speech was the final betrayal.
- **Daniel:** After 8 years of loyal service, Harold was going to fire Daniel and frame him for an FDA investigation. Daniel wanted Harold to know what betrayal felt like.

# The True Horror

## The Real Story

The murder mystery is a trap. The real story is about Thomas Vance - a man betrayed by his partner, entombed alive, transformed by suffering into something inhuman, and now fused with the very walls of Vance Manor.

## The Cycle

Every investigator who comes to solve the murder eventually learns too much. They find Arthur's tapes. They discover the truth about Thomas. And in doing so, they draw the Entity's attention.

The Narrator guiding you was once an investigator too. They failed. They died. Now they exist only to guide the next investigator through the same doomed investigation.

## The Entity's Nature

Thomas Vance was entombed alive in 1957. For 30 years, he remained trapped behind the study wall. Through decades of suffering, he transformed. He became something no longer human. He fused with the house itself.

When Arthur opened the wall in 1987, he didn't find a victim to rescue. He found something that had been waiting. Something that had learned to sound human. Something that used Arthur's compassion against him.

Arthur died because he tried to do the right thing.

## The Awareness System

The Awareness Meter represents Thomas becoming aware of your presence. Every question, every examination, every discovery draws his attention. At 80 awareness, he fully awakens. The investigation ends. You become the next Narrator.

## Will You Break the Cycle?

You can solve the murder. You can identify James and Daniel as the killers. But in doing so, you'll learn about Thomas. You'll find Arthur's tapes. You'll discover the truth.

And the Entity will be watching.

Will you break the cycle? Or will you become the next Narrator?
